🇮🇳 Famous Places in India

India is full of amazing monuments, temples and natural wonders. Here are six places you should not miss!

Jump to a place:

  • Taj Mahal
  • Red Fort
  • Hawa Mahal
  • Golden Temple
  • Gateway of India
  • Kerala Backwaters

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

The Taj Mahal is a white marble monument built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. It is one of the Seven Wonders of the World and is loved for its beautiful symmetry.

Fun fact: It took around 20 years to build.

Back to Top

2. Red Fort, Delhi

The Red Fort is a huge fort made of red sandstone. Every year on 15 August, the Prime Minister hoists the national flag here on Independence Day.

Fun fact: The fort was the main home of the Mughal emperors for almost 200 years.

Back to Top

3. Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

Hawa Mahal is also called the "Palace of Winds". It has hundreds of small windows that let cool air pass through, and it glows pink in the sunlight.

Fun fact: It was built so that royal ladies could watch street life without being seen.

Back to Top

4. Golden Temple, Amritsar

The Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib) is the holiest place of Sikhism. It shines with gold and is surrounded by a sacred pool. Its community kitchen serves free food to thousands of people every day.

Fun fact: Visitors of every religion are welcome here.

Back to Top

5. Gateway of India, Mumbai

The Gateway of India is a grand stone arch standing next to the Arabian Sea. It was built to remember the visit of King George V and Queen Mary to Mumbai.

Fun fact: From here you can take a boat ride to the Elephanta Caves.

Back to Top

6. Kerala Backwaters, Kerala

The Kerala backwaters are a peaceful network of lakes, canals and rivers lined with coconut palms. Many visitors stay on a traditional houseboat and enjoy the calm green scenery.

Fun fact: The area is also known for its delicious fresh fish and rice dishes.
